I've been a VR enthusiast for quite a long time. Longer than any other person I know, at least. I purchased an Oculus Developer Kit 2 back in June of 2014.

Virtual Reality has changed a lot as a medium since then.

I really wasn't myself then.

Sometime in late December of the year 2020, I was casually scrolling through a collection of gaming-related subreddits, casually procrastinating doing any actual work for the evening, before coming across a post that really caught my eye. It was describing some sort of Japanese virtual convention that went by the name 'VKet' and was being hosted on something called 'VRChat' It promised a wide selection of virtual goods ranging from avatars, avatar accessories.

I had heard of VRChat a number of times during the years between but I pretty well ignored its existence. I wasn't much of a social person. The thought of going out of my way to socialize with people felt taxing and unproductive. I much preferred to hole myself up in my room and work on over-scoped game dev projects about colorful equines.

This time though, it grabbed my attention. This VKet world looked really detailed and well-crafted. Something I wasn't previously exposed to in any of the prior VRChat media I've seen.

So I opened Steam, installed VRChat, strapped on my then-primary headset, an Oculus Quest 2, and hopped right in.

The world was indeed very pretty and cool to experience. There were in fact avatars and assets there for sale. I wasn't quite hooked but it was interesting enough. So I stayed for awhile, somewhat avoiding the 'Chat' part of the platform's title unless directly confronted. I was a bit shy back then.

After a few hours of that, I felt like I had my fill and I hopped off. It was neat, sure, but I wasn't really feeling the hook. The interactivity felt super limited, the interface was kind of ugly, and everything just felt a little bit jank. The worlds were cool though so I kept that in mind in case I got bored of my single-player VR games and wanted to just experience some on-demand visual content.

Fast forward to early February, 2021, an exchange with a friend of mine on Discord. He off-handedly asks if I play VRChat. 'Just once' I say. He extends a casual invite to play with him and his friend group but I decide I'm far too busy with work to hop on. He repeats a couple times over the next month or two but I continue to decline; although it certainly kept the game fresh in my mind over that time.

Finally in early April, after seeing a handful of YouTube videos and a particular Twitch streamer showcasing an avatar called 'The Awtter' while dancing to an assortment of EDM, I finally got intrigued enough to try it again. The Awtter was cute. Really, really cute. I was absolutely locked-in on this streamer as she spun around, shook her hips, and just threw it back to classic S3RL tracks. I was completely mesmerized for the next hour. At some point during that hour, something switches in my brain. I wasn't just enjoying looking at this cute otter dance. 

I wanted to *be* that otter. Really badly.

Now at that time, I wouldn't have really considered myself a Furry. I was familiar with the culture and art, occasionally browsed it myself. I had a few Furry friends but I was firmly what you would call a 'Brony'.

But I really, *really* wanted to be that otter. It was like 4AM at this point but I felt *a compulsion*.

So I pulled out my headset and start VRChat again for the first time in a couple months. I immediately search for the public version of The Awtter, pick out the blue one, and equip it as fast as I can. I look in the nearby mirror.

Holy shit.
I'm cute.

I wiggle around a bit and try out the various expressions. I move my head and limbs in a variety of poses. I play with the finger positioning that causes my face to emote into various, adorable expressions. There was a sort of euphoria in these actions and seeing myself represented this way.

I'm getting tired though and I have to cook dinner soon so I begrudgingly hop off. However, my mind remained plagued with possibility throughout the evening and the next day.
